# TimeTrail v0.4 — Map & Gameplay Experience

## Objective
Improve the physical feel and clarity of the 1900 historical atlas without changing the 50-question bank, scoring rules, or the daily ten-journey rotation. All in-game copy remains English.

## Implemented in this branch

1. **Map camera** — wheel zoom keeps the point beneath the cursor stationary, zoom buttons preserve the map centre, and the reset control reframes city candidates during an active city challenge.
2. **Mobile map interaction** — two-finger pinch-to-zoom, cancellation of accidental answers after multi-touch, and drag-to-pan retained.
3. **City navigation** — hide competing capital stars while selecting cities; make wrong city choices visually identifiable without penalizing repeat taps; larger, more legible map markers.
4. **Travel feedback** — leg/chapter language, a progress bar, and a clearer animated route.
5. **Question and results** — distinct challenge labels, accurate earned-progress indicator, explicit historical-answer feedback, and a five-destination score breakdown.
6. **Visual and accessibility polish** — refreshed atlas palette, stronger contrast, motion-reduction accommodations, accessible progress labels, and responsive map-gesture guidance.
7. **Regression coverage** — existing full-journey and content tests plus additional camera, release, and UI-regression checks in `tests/experience.test.cjs`.

## Land and sea travel improvement (added after playtesting)
- Replaced straight-line travel with a dedicated `travel-routes.js` catalogue of schematic land and sea corridors.
- All 50 daily-journey legs resolve to a curated route. The route engine disallows missing direct-line fallbacks.
- Land legs animate a train, sea legs animate a boat. The route is broken into waypoints instead of cutting straight across major bodies of water.
- Previously travelled corridors remain visible in the correct land/sea styling rather than reverting to direct lines.
- `tests/routes.test.cjs` validates all 50 legs, endpoints, known ferry/water crossings, reverse paths, and the animation wiring.
- These corridors are illustrative and must not be presented as historical railway tracks or verified sea timetables.
- A manual geographic QA pass against the full 1900 GeoJSON coastline is still required; the automated tests verify route structure, not every metre of land-water intersection.

## Verification / acceptance criteria
- `node --check app.js && node --check content.js && node --check travel-routes.js`
- `node --test tests/*.test.cjs`
- Country/city selection and quiz scoring are unchanged; navigation clicks are not counted during pinch or pan.
- Zooming via mouse wheel maintains the geographic location beneath the pointer.
- The standard five-stop journeys still complete with accurate scoring, answers, and saved progress.
- All visible copy stays in English.
- GitHub Pages remains on v0.3 until this pull request is reviewed and merged.
